The Salon Story

A Decade of Mastering the Fine Art of Nail Architecture

Founded in 2014 by master nail artist Uma Sharma, Nails by Uma started with a single conviction: nail care is not just cosmetic maintenance, but an intimate form of wearable art and personal confidence.

Today, our salon serves over 5,000+ loyal clients each year, offering certified non-toxic European gel polishes, autoclave hospital-grade sterilization, and customized mobile home salon services throughout Jaipur.
